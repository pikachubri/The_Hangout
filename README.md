The Hangout is a Spotify inspired Music Application designed to bring music to those in a stress free virtual environment.
Users will be able to register their accounts and login and create playlists, browse songs and listen to the radio.
Users will be able to listen to music at their own pace in a controlled environment through volume and playback control.
Features:
Registration
Login
Playlist management
Radio station with multiple stations and genres
Browse and filter songs
Architecture overview
HTML
CSS
JavaScript

Pages
Index/home page (radio station/ let the music choose you here for when you're indecisive)
Browse (find and filter music/discover songs)
Playlist  (all your favorites in one place/ pick what you want to play from a selection of your own songs with a queue system.)
About me (more about the website)
